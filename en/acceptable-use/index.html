Acceptable Use and Prohibited Activities (BeriPay)

These rules supplement the Public Offer https://beripay.net/terms and the AML/CFT Policy https://beripay.net/aml. Use of the “BeriPay” (“Бери и плати”) Service is permitted only for lawful payments outside the Russian Federation with the Virtual Card within the spend-only product model.

It is prohibited to use the Service for: financing of terrorism and extremism; financing of the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction; money laundering and concealment of the origin of funds; circumvention of sanctions; fraud, carding, purchase of stolen data; illicit trafficking in drugs and weapons; human trafficking and sexual exploitation of minors; unlicensed gambling and betting where prohibited by law or by partner rules; cash withdrawal / ATM, withdrawal to bank accounts and other transactions circumventing the Offer; operating through nominee accounts and transferring access to third parties.

The Service may refuse service or restrict transactions in the following areas, without limitation: adult content and related payouts; activity related to unlicensed FX/investments and promises of guaranteed returns; crypto exchange activity beyond the permitted top-up methods; high-risk MCCs under the rules of the issuing partner; transactions with persons and in jurisdictions on sanctions and restrictive lists.

The User is obliged to undergo identity verification at the request of the Service. Refusal to undergo verification, false data or suspicious activity constitute grounds for restricting or terminating access.
