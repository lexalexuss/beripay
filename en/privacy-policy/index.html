Policy on the Processing of Personal Data of Users of the BeriPay Service

1. General Provisions

1.1. This Policy (hereinafter, the “Policy”) governs matters relating to the processing of the personal data of Users (hereinafter, the “Personal Data Subject”) by NextLab LLC (ООО «НекстЛаб»), Registration No. 326188-3301-ООО, TIN (INN) 00203202610365, address: Office 4a, 141 Toktogula St., Pervomaisky District, Bishkek, Kyrgyz Republic (hereinafter, the “Operator”), in connection with the Users’ use of the Website https://beripay.net, the application and the Telegram chatbot https://t.me/BeriPay_bot (hereinafter collectively, the “BeriPay” (“Бери и плати”) Service), which are intended for the issuance of a Virtual Card of an international payment system to Users and its use by them.

The person responsible for Personal Data Processing, for ensuring the security of personal data and for establishing the relevant structural unit for personal data security is the sole executive body of the Operator.

1.2. When the User uses the BeriPay Service, the Operator processes the personal data of Personal Data Subjects, namely performs any and all actions with personal data, including collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, rectification (updating, modification), retrieval, use, transfer (dissemination, provision, access), depersonalisation, blocking, deletion and destruction of personal data (hereinafter, “Personal Data Processing”).

1.3. The personal data of the User processed under this Policy include: surname, first name and patronymic (if any); day, month and year of birth; email address; telephone number; registration address; details of the identity document (passport series and number, issuing authority and date of issue, etc.); bank card details; settlement account number; TIN (INN); cookies; and other data that may be obtained in the course of the User’s use of the BeriPay Service.

1.4. The Operator acts as the Administrator of the BeriPay Service and the Website.

1.5. All requests, demands and claims addressed to the Operator (hereinafter, “Requests”) shall be sent in the form of an electronic image of a document in PDF format bearing a graphic image of the signature of the Personal Data Subject or their representative (together with a power of attorney or another document confirming the representative’s authority). A Request must also contain the number of the principal identity document of the Personal Data Subject or their representative, information on the date of issue of that document and the issuing authority, and information confirming the Personal Data Subject’s participation in relations with the Operator (agreement number, date of conclusion of the agreement, conventional verbal designation and/or other information), or information otherwise confirming the fact of personal data processing by the Operator. Requests shall be sent to support: https://t.me/BeriPay_SupportBot.

1.6. Before commencing the cross-border transfer of personal data, the Operator shall notify the authorised body for the protection of the rights of personal data subjects of its intention to carry out cross-border transfers of personal data.

2. Purposes of Personal Data Processing

2.1. The purpose of processing the User’s personal data is the conclusion and performance of the Agreement (the Public Offer on the Use of the BeriPay Information Technology Service for Payments Outside the Russian Federation), ensuring the User’s use of the functionality of the BeriPay Service, including registration of an Account (Personal Account), provision of access to the functionality of the Service and to the Virtual Card issued by the issuing partner / card program operator, and conducting statistical or other research to enable the use, further development and improvement of the BeriPay Service.

2.2. Personal data may be used for other purposes only where this is mandatory under the provisions of the legislation of the Russian Federation.

2.3. Personal data processing shall be limited to the achievement of specific, predetermined and lawful purposes. Processing of personal data that is incompatible with the purposes for which the personal data were collected is not permitted.

3. Legal Grounds for Personal Data Processing

The legal grounds for Personal Data Processing under this Policy are:

  • the Consent to the Processing of Personal Data of a User of the BeriPay Service;

  • the Agreement (the Public Offer on the Use of the BeriPay Information Technology Service for Payments Outside the Russian Federation);

  • Federal Law No. 152-FZ “On Personal Data” of 27 July 2006, other federal laws and subordinate regulatory acts.

4. Scope and Categories of Personal Data Processed; Categories of Personal Data Subjects

4.1. The personal data specified in clause 1.3 of this Policy are processed to the extent necessary to achieve the purposes set out in Section 2 of this Policy.

4.2. The Operator processes the following categories of personal data of Users: personal data of the general category.

4.3. The Operator guarantees that the personal data processed are not excessive in relation to the stated purposes of their processing.

5. Procedure and Conditions for Personal Data Processing

5.1. Actions performed by the Operator with personal data: collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, rectification (updating, modification), retrieval, use, transfer (dissemination, provision, access), depersonalisation, blocking, deletion, destruction.

5.2. Personal data are processed by the Operator using automated systems, except where non-automated processing of personal data is necessary in order to comply with the requirements of the legislation of the Russian Federation.

5.3. Processing of personal data commences immediately upon their receipt and continues for the period necessary to perform the terms of the Agreement or until the Consent is withdrawn.

5.4. The Operator may transfer personal data to bodies of inquiry and investigation and to other authorised bodies on the grounds provided for by the applicable legislation of the Russian Federation.

5.5. The Operator guarantees compliance with the personal data confidentiality requirements established by Article 7 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ “On Personal Data” of 27 July 2006, as well as the adoption of the measures provided for by Part 2 of Article 18.1 and Part 1 of Article 19 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ “On Personal Data” of 27 July 2006.

5.6. The conditions for the termination by the Operator of the processing of the personal data of Personal Data Subjects are the withdrawal by the Personal Data Subject of consent to the Processing of their personal data, as well as the detection of unlawful processing of personal data.

5.7. Personal data are stored in a form that makes it possible to identify the Personal Data Subject, for the periods that are necessary for the performance of the obligations provided for by the Agreement and that are specified in the Consent to the processing of personal data.

5.8. When storing personal data, the Operator uses databases located in the territory of the Russian Federation. The personal data of Personal Data Subjects are stored exclusively on electronic media.

5.9. The Operator takes technical, organisational and legal measures to protect the personal data of Personal Data Subjects against unlawful or accidental access, destruction, modification, blocking, copying and dissemination, as well as against other unlawful actions.

To access the Account, the Personal Data Subject uses individual means of authentication.

The Personal Data Subject is responsible for keeping this information safe.

The Personal Data Subject may not grant access to the Account to third parties and shall take measures to ensure its confidentiality.

5.10. The Operator may engage third parties to process personal data to the extent necessary for the operation of the Service, including the issuing partner / card program operator, payment providers, identity verification (KYC/AML) providers and, where crypto top-up is enabled, blockchain analytics providers. The transfer is carried out on the basis of an agreement and for the purposes of performing the Offer and complying with the requirements of partners and applicable law. The right of the Personal Data Subject to access their personal data may be restricted in accordance with federal laws, including where: (1) Personal Data Processing, including of personal data obtained as a result of operational search, counterintelligence and intelligence activities, is carried out for the purposes of national defence, state security and the maintenance of law and order; (2) Personal Data Processing is carried out by bodies that have detained the Personal Data Subject on suspicion of having committed a crime, or have charged the Personal Data Subject in a criminal case, or have applied a preventive measure to the Personal Data Subject before bringing charges, except in the cases provided for by the criminal procedure legislation of the Russian Federation where the suspect or accused is permitted to access such personal data; (3) Personal Data Processing is carried out in accordance with the legislation on combating the legalisation (laundering) of criminally obtained proceeds and the financing of terrorism; (4) access by the Personal Data Subject to their personal data violates the rights and legitimate interests of third parties; (5) Personal Data Processing is carried out in the cases provided for by the legislation of the Russian Federation on transport security, for the purposes of ensuring the stable and safe functioning of the transport complex and protecting the interests of the individual, society and the state in the transport sector against acts of unlawful interference.

If the inaccuracy of the personal data is confirmed, the Operator shall, on the basis of the information provided by the Personal Data Subject or their representative or by the authorised body for the protection of the rights of personal data subjects, or of other necessary documents, rectify the personal data or ensure that they are rectified (if the processing of personal data is carried out by another person acting on behalf of the Operator) within seven working days from the date on which such information is provided, and lift the blocking of the personal data.

If it is impossible to ensure the lawfulness of Personal Data Processing, the Operator shall, within a period not exceeding 10 working days from the date of detection of the unlawful processing of personal data, destroy such personal data or ensure their destruction.

The Operator shall notify the Personal Data Subject or their representative of the elimination of the violations committed or of the destruction of the personal data and, where the application of the Personal Data Subject or their representative or the request of the authorised body for the protection of the rights of personal data subjects was sent by the authorised body for the protection of the rights of personal data subjects, shall also notify that body.

6.9. If the Personal Data Subject withdraws consent to the processing of their personal data, the Operator shall cease processing them or ensure that such processing is ceased (if the processing of personal data is carried out by another person acting on behalf of the Operator) and, if the retention of the personal data is no longer required for the purposes of Personal Data Processing, destroy the personal data or ensure their destruction (if the processing of personal data is carried out by another person acting on behalf of the Operator) within a period not exceeding 30 days from the date of receipt of such withdrawal, unless otherwise provided for by an agreement to which the Personal Data Subject is a party, beneficiary or guarantor, or by another agreement between the Operator and the Personal Data Subject, or if the Operator is not entitled to process personal data without the consent of the Personal Data Subject on the grounds provided for by Federal Law No. 152-FZ “On Personal Data” of 27 July 2006 or other federal laws.

That period may be extended, but by no more than 5 working days, if the Operator sends the Personal Data Subject a reasoned notice stating the reasons for extending the period for providing the requested information.
