Definitions

“User” means an individual having full legal capacity in accordance with the applicable law who has, by their conduct, performed actions aimed at concluding the Agreement for the provision of services by accepting the terms of this Offer, and who holds authentication credentials for accessing the Service in order to use it.

“Service” means the software owned by the Rightholder through which the service of making payments outside the Russian Federation is provided by means of a mobile application and a chatbot in Telegram https://t.me/BeriPay_bot.

“Personal Account (PA)” means a section of the Service created to display information for the User, including details of settlements, the balance, notifications, statistics, reports and other information in real time.

“Virtual Card” means a payment instrument to which the User is granted access through the Service in the amount of the top-up for payments outside the Russian Federation. The Virtual Card is issued and serviced by an issuing partner / card program operator engaged by the Rightholder. It is not a bank card of the User opened with the User’s bank. The Rightholder and the Administrator are not a bank and do not act as a payment system. The card details and the terms of use of the card are determined by the rules of the issuing partner and this Offer.

“Transfer” means actions of the User using the Service to make settlements outside the Russian Federation.

“Recurring Payment” means the automatic debiting of funds from the User’s saved account to top up the balance of the Virtual Card when funds are insufficient.

4.3. Funds are converted into US dollars (USD) or euros (EUR) for the purposes of their accounting / crediting to the balance account of the Virtual Card at the internal exchange rate of the Service.
